Where should a partial ring sit?

Side view of one tip: flow left → right (leading edge to trailing edge). Gray is today’s full-span hoop. Accent is a ~30% band. Default stays full-span; this only matters when the height knob is below 1.0.

A — From the trailing edge

Band covers the rear ~30% of the tip chord. Common on loaded axial fans: leak and unloading are strongest near the TE. Recommended.

B — Centered on mid-chord

Band sits in the middle of the tip Z-span. Neutral, no LE/TE bias. Leaves both ends of the chord open to leak.

C — From the leading edge

Band covers the front ~30%. Can trip the inlet and leave the TE (where the tip vortex dumps) uncovered.
