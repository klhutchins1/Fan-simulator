## How the parts were read

A B-rep `isInside` probe on the Stock solid ran about 20 minutes and never returned a hub face. It was killed. No classification from that probe is used. The read is a horizontal section of the exported STL.

Stock was tessellated at the real print tolerance, 0.02 mm / 0.1 rad. t9002 was tessellated at 0.05 mm / 0.2 rad. OCCT reported the Stock solid watertight, one body. Trimesh, loaded without welding vertices, does not call that STL watertight; that is the loader, not a second body.

Two instrument bugs showed up before the numbers below were accepted. Neither one changed a threshold.

- The 0.05 mm re-probe searched the axis-aligned box of the "7". That box also held 48 points of the neighbouring K, 2.2–2.8 mm from the 7. Those points made the bar look 4.23 mm thick and the hang 0.70 mm. The probe now keeps points within the 0.62 mm link already used to form a blob. Every point of the 7 is within 0.40 mm of that blob; the K points are not. A dense analytic 7, placed on the hub arc and never cut into a mesh, measures 0.862 mm and hang 3.245 mm with the same estimator. The flat 0.9 mm control is 0.900 mm (inside the window) and the 0.6 mm control is 0.600 mm (outside it).
- The cup-band vertex filter saw zero samples. A cylinder face is tessellated only on its end rings, and both rings sit outside z ∈ (hub z0 + 0.5, hub z0 + 12). The written sample was vertices in the slab plus edge quarter-points. Quarter-points that land in the slab are what was measured. The first result was "no samples", not a diameter of 36.55.
