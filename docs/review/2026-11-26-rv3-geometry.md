## 3. First-Principles Sign & Coordinate Verification Summary

In `fansim/geometry/blade.py` (all lengths in mm, angles in degrees converted via `np.radians`):

1. **Airfoil frame (`airfoil.py`):** $x \in [0, 1]$ (LE at $0$, TE at $1$), upper surface $y_u = y_c + y_t\cos\theta$, lower surface $y_l = y_c - y_t\cos\theta$. For $m > 0$, the camber line bows toward $+y$, so $+b$ is the **convex (suction)** face and $-b$ is the **concave (pressure)** face.
2. **Stagger and LE-leads transform (`blade.py:441-446`):**
   - For $\beta = \text{stagger\_deg} \ge 0$: $a = (0.5 - x_{\text{surf}}) c$ (LE at $+c/2$, TE at $-c/2$).
   - Tangential and axial coordinates:
     $$t = a\cos\beta - b\sin\beta + \text{skew} + \text{le\_skew}\,w_{\text{LE}}(x), \qquad z = a\sin\beta + b\cos\beta + \text{sweep} + \text{le\_sweep}\,w_{\text{LE}}(x) + z_{\text{blade}}.$$
   - Chord vector (TE $\to$ LE) is $(\Delta t, \Delta z) = (c\cos\beta, c\sin\beta)$, making angle $\beta$ with the rotation plane; LE sits at $(+t, +Z)$.
   - Concave pressure-face normal points along $(+\sin\beta, -\cos\beta)$ — pushing incoming $+Z$ air toward $-Z$ under $\omega_z = +1$ (CCW from $+Z$, blades advancing in $+t$), matching `geometry.stock_handedness` (`omega_sign=+1, pump_sign=-1, chirality=-1`).
   - For $\beta < 0$ (mirrored / CROR rear via `mirror_blade_params`): $a = (x_{\text{surf}} - 0.5)c$, yielding exact reflection $(t, z) \mapsto (-t, +z)$ so LE leads in $-t$ (for $\omega_z = -1$) while still pumping $-Z$.
3. **Cylinder wrap vs tangent plane (`blade.py:449-461`):**
   - `wrap=True`: $\theta = \theta_{\text{lean}} + t/r$, $(x, y) = (r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta)$ — exact arc length $t$ on cylinder radius $r > 0$.
   - `wrap=False`: $(x, y) = (r\cos\theta_{\text{lean}} - t\sin\theta_{\text{lean}}, r\sin\theta_{\text{lean}} + t\cos\theta_{\text{lean}})$ — exact Euclidean tangent plane at radius $r$.
