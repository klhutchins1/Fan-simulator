# RV-3 Module 8 Review — Cockpit (`fansim/cockpit/`)

Reviewed 2026-11-26 against the RV-3 checklist in `docs/review/2026-11-24-review-plan.md`
(54 modules, ~15.5k lines: UI layout, session state, background job orchestration, 3-D visualization,
placement/fit helpers, and score provenance).

## Checklist summary

1. **Equations, units, and signs**
   - The cockpit contains no standalone physics solvers; all aero, structural, and acoustic
     calculations delegate to `fansim.fan_bem`, `fansim.propeller.bemt`, `fansim.cfd`,
     `fansim.structural`, `fansim.acoustics`, `fansim.fan_spec`, and `fansim.geometry`.
   - Like-for-like Stock comparison (`vs_stock.py`) enforces identical `fan_model`, `preset`,
     `load_pa`, `refine_op`, `material`, and roughness regime before computing `% free air`, and
     never compares scored CFD against datasheet CFM.
   - **Finding C1 (medium — fixed here):** `fansim/cockpit/placement.py:_blade_z_bounds` called
     `fansim.geometry.blade._section_points_3d` without `te_face` / `le_face` and used falsy-zero
     `float(getattr(params, "le_thick_scale", 1.0) or 1.0)` (treating `0.0` plate roundness as
     `1.0`). Fixed by reusing `fansim.geometry.blade._thick_scale_kw(params)` and forwarding
     interpolated `te_face` and `le_face`, matching the actual blade loft.

2. **Silent exception handling**
   - Audited all `except` blocks across `fansim/cockpit/*.py`.
   - User-triggered actions (Preview, Assess, Optimize, Sweep, Export, Refine) surface errors via
     `state.*_msg` (`⚠ ...`) or `fansim/cockpit/errors.py` (`log_caught` / `set_state_error`).
   - Optional UI/cleanup guards (Trame `state.flush()` / `view_update()` during disconnect,
     PyVista optional overlays, cache-stamp reads, corrupt saved `design.json` skips) are either
     explicitly logged or intentionally non-fatal with user-visible fallback text.
   - **Finding C2 (medium — fixed here):** `fansim/cockpit/fan_assess.py:spec_status` caught any
     exception while reading the preview STL (`trimesh.load` / volume calculation) and silently
     returned `(True, "")`, presenting a failed check identically to an in-spec build. Fixed to
     log via `log_caught` and return an explicit `⚠ Could not verify build spec from preview STL`
     warning.

3. **Physical constants single source**
   - Grepped all 54 cockpit modules for hardcoded air/unit constants (`2118.88`, `1.204`, `1.225`,
     `343`, `1.5e-5`, `1.81e-5`, `9.81`): zero duplicates found.

4. **UX & live-browser consistency (headless Chrome audit)**
   - **Finding C3 (UX — fixed here):** `fansim/cockpit/fan_tab_ui.py` left `⏹ Cancel viz`
     visible while idle on Fan → Results (Expert mode), unlike `⏹ Cancel opt`, `⏹ Cancel Assess`,
     Sweep `⏹ Cancel`, and Prop `Stop`. Added `v_show="airflow_busy"` so zero idle cancel/stop
     buttons appear anywhere in the cockpit.
   - **Finding C4 (UX — fixed here):** `fansim/cockpit/mixed_tab.py` omitted `EXPERIMENTAL_LABEL`
     on its top Design card, whereas `volute_tab.py` and `cror_tab.py` both display it prominently
     at the top of their Design cards. Added the matching `EXPERIMENTAL_LABEL` subtitle to Mixed.

5. **Audit ledger & numeric tests**
   - Cockpit modules are UI/orchestration and exempt from the physics audit ledger (`audit_status.py`
     tracks physics/geometry/CFD/structural/acoustics/optimize units).
   - Pure cockpit helpers (`placement.py`, `vs_stock.py`, `score_caption.py`, `input_checks.py`,
     `library_view.py`, `design_names.py`, `fan_widgets.py`, `fan_helpers.py`) have direct unit
     tests (`tests/test_ux_live_audit.py`, `tests/test_rv3_cockpit.py`, `tests/test_ui_says_what_it_does.py`,
     and placement/cockpit suites).

6. **Function length (>150 lines)**
   - Large UI builder / binder functions remain in `fan_tab_ui.py` (`build_fan_tab`, ~1750 lines),
     `prop_tab.py`, `mixed_tab.py`, `volute_tab.py`, `cror_tab.py`, `fan_viz.py`, `fan_editor.py`,
     and `fan_assess.py`.
   - Per RV-5 (`docs/review/2026-11-24-review-plan.md`), cockpit `bind`/`render` splits are
     deferred until UX work touches those sections directly.
