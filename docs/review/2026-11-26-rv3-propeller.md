## 3. First-Principles Derivation Summary (Reference)

For an open propeller of $B$ blades, tip radius $R$, hub radius $r_{\text{hub}}$, rotational speed $\omega = 2\pi N/60$, forward speed $V_\infty \ge 0$, and air density $\rho$, consider an annular blade element at radius $r \in (r_{\text{hub}}, R)$ of width $dr$, chord $c(r)$, and pitch angle $\beta(r)$ measured from the plane of rotation:

1. **Velocity triangle at the actuator disk** (Glauert 1935; Leishman 2006 §3.3; Drela QPROP 2006):
   $$V_{\text{ax}} = V_\infty + v_i, \qquad V_t = \Omega r - v_{\theta,m} = \Omega r (1 - a'), \quad v_{\theta,m} = \tfrac{1}{2}v_\theta,$$
   $$\phi = \operatorname{atan2}(V_{\text{ax}}, V_t), \qquad W = \sqrt{V_{\text{ax}}^2 + V_t^2}, \qquad \alpha = \beta - \phi, \qquad \text{Re} = \frac{W c}{\nu}, \qquad M = \frac{W}{c_0}.$$
2. **Blade-element forces per unit span (all $B$ blades):**
   $$dT' = \tfrac{1}{2}\rho W^2 c B (C_l\cos\phi - C_d\sin\phi), \qquad f_\theta = \tfrac{1}{2}\rho W^2 c B (C_l\sin\phi + C_d\cos\phi), \qquad dQ' = r\,f_\theta.$$
3. **Prandtl finite-blade tip/hub loss** (Prandtl 1919; Glauert 1935; Leishman 2006 Eqs. 3.76–3.77):
   $$f_{\text{tip}} = \frac{B}{2}\frac{R - r}{r\sin|\phi|}, \qquad F_{\text{tip}} = \frac{2}{\pi}\arccos\!\left(e^{-f_{\text{tip}}}\right), \qquad f_{\text{hub}} = \frac{B}{2}\frac{r - r_{\text{hub}}}{r\sin|\phi|}, \qquad F_{\text{hub}} = \frac{2}{\pi}\arccos\!\left(e^{-f_{\text{hub}}}\right), \qquad F = F_{\text{tip}} F_{\text{hub}}.$$
   *(Code uses $F_{\text{tip}}$ floored at `0.15`, without $F_{\text{hub}}$.)*
4. **Axial & angular momentum balance** (Glauert 1935; Adkins & Liebeck 1994 Eqs. 13–14):
   With circumferentially averaged induced velocities $\bar{v}_i = F v_i$ and $\bar{v}_\theta = F v_\theta$:
   $$dT' = (2\pi r \rho |V_{\text{ax}}|)(2 F v_i) = 4\pi r \rho |V_{\text{ax}}| v_i F, \qquad f_\theta = (2\pi r \rho |V_{\text{ax}}|)(F v_\theta) = 2\pi r \rho |V_{\text{ax}}| v_\theta F.$$
   *(Code omits $F$ from the $f_\theta$ denominator and clamps $v_\theta \ge 0$ in windmilling — tracked in [#109](https://github.com/klhutchins1/Fan-simulator/issues/109).)*
5. **Integrated performance & nondimensional propeller coefficients** (Leishman 2006 Eq. 2.40; Glauert 1935):
   $$T = \int_{r_{\text{hub}}}^{R} dT'\,dr, \qquad Q = \int_{r_{\text{hub}}}^{R} r f_\theta\,dr, \qquad P = Q\omega, \qquad T_g = \frac{T}{g_0}\times 1000,$$
   $$\text{FM}_{\text{hover}} = \frac{T^{3/2}/\sqrt{2\rho (\pi R^2)}}{P}, \qquad J = \frac{V_\infty}{n D}, \qquad C_T = \frac{T}{\rho n^2 D^4}, \qquad C_P = \frac{P}{\rho n^3 D^5}, \qquad \eta_p = \frac{T V_\infty}{P} = \frac{J C_T}{C_P}.$$
